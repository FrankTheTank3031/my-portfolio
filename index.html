About Me

A designer who codes, a developer who designs.

I'm Frank Vitali, a full-stack designer and developer based in New York City. With over a decade of experience, I've helped startups and established brands transform their digital presence through thoughtful design systems and robust engineering.

My philosophy is simple: technology should feel effortless. Every pixel, every interaction, every line of code serves the person using it. I bridge the gap between creative vision and technical execution, ensuring nothing gets lost in translation.

When I'm not shipping products, you'll find me exploring typography, contributing to open-source projects, or somewhere in the mountains with a camera in hand.
